Privacy Policy for PDF Watch Go

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Nikita Mirashnichenka ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our iOS and watchOS application, PDF Watch Go (the "Service").

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency. Our core document processing functions operate strictly on your personal devices without storing your documents on any external servers.

2. Document Processing & File Storage (Your PDFs)

Zero Server Storage: Your PDF files and converted document pages are never uploaded to, transmitted through, or stored on our servers or any cloud services.
  • On-Device Conversion: When you select a document, PDF pages are converted into image files locally on your iPhone using Apple's built-in frameworks.
  • Direct Peer-to-Peer Transfer: Files are transferred directly between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch using Apple's standard encrypted WatchConnectivity protocol.
  • Automatic Local Cleanup: Temporary page files generated during transfer are automatically purged from your iPhone's temporary cache shortly after transmission. You can also wipe all transferred pages from your Apple Watch at any time via the "Clear" button in the app.

3. Information Collected via Third-Party Services

We do not require account registration, and we do not directly collect personal identifying details such as your name, email address, or physical address. However, to support our free version and monitor application stability, the app integrates third-party SDKs provided by Google LLC.

3.1 Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads in the free version of the app. Google may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising identifier (IDFA) on iOS, if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Usage Data: Information regarding ad interactions, views, taps, and app launch timestamps.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: SDK error logs, crash metrics, and load times to assess advertising delivery and stability.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms Partner Site and Google Privacy Policy.

3.2 In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (Apple StoreKit)

We offer optional auto-renewable subscriptions to unlock premium functionality (e.g., unlimited transfers and ad-free experience). All transactions are processed solely by Apple Inc. through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card numbers, banking details, or billing addresses. We only receive anonymized transaction entitlements from Apple's StoreKit framework to verify whether your subscription is active.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our processing of your personal data relies on the following legal bases under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): For serving personalized advertisements and accessing your device identifier (IDFA). We obtain this via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog and Apple's ATT prompt.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): For serving non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, app security, and troubleshooting diagnostic errors.
  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To validate your purchases and deliver premium subscription features requested by you.

5. Managing Your Choices & Withdrawing Consent

You maintain full control over your privacy preferences at any time:

  • In-App Privacy Settings (UMP): If you reside in the EEA or UK, you can review or change your advertising consent at any time by tapping the (?) guide button in the app and selecting Privacy Settings.
  • Apple Tracking Transparency (ATT): You can revoke or grant tracking permissions at any time under your iOS settings: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > PDF Watch Go.
  • Apple Personalized Ads: You can manage Apple-delivered ads in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

6.1 European Union & UK Residents (GDPR)

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.
  • Lodge a complaint with a Data Protection Authority (DPA) in your country of residence.

6.2 California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA):

  • We do not sell personal information in the traditional monetary sense. However, sharing identifiers (like IDFA) for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" or "selling" under California law.
  • You have the right to opt out of the sharing/sale of personal information (exercised via ATT / tracking opt-out).
  • You have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal data collected and request deletion.
  • We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
  • We do not collect or process "Sensitive Personal Information" as defined under the CPRA.

7. Device Permissions

The application requests access strictly to necessary system components:

  • Document Picker: To allow you to browse and select the PDF files you choose to import. The app operates inside Apple's security sandbox and cannot read other files on your device.
  • Tracking Permission: Requested via Apple's standard ATT prompt to deliver personalized advertising. Granting this permission is completely optional.

8. International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers (such as Google) may transfer, store, and process information in the United States and other countries outside your country of residence. These transfers are safeguarded by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or equivalent adequacy mechanisms.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.
